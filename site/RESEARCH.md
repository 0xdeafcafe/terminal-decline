# Terminal website research

Read 7 November 2026. These are design judgements from the linked pages, not allegations that the products are bad. The inherited anti-slop research remains in ANTI-SLOP.md.

| Neighbour | What the page leads with | Risk for Decline | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/) and its [own README](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty) | Speed, features, native UI and GPU rendering | A substitute-logo headline fails to distinguish another native terminal | Lead with the dated speed and footprint measurements, as Alex confirmed. Pins and ctl follow. No mascot-led imitation. |
| [Warp Terminal](https://www.warp.dev/terminal) and [Warp](https://www.warp.dev/) | Agent work plus a broader software-factory product, enterprise navigation and demo calls | A visitor wanting a Mac terminal must separate the terminal from the platform pitch | Show exactly what ctl does. No AI-platform promises, logo wall or sales funnel. |
| [WezTerm](https://wezterm.org/) | A cross-platform terminal/multiplexer within a substantial documentation navigation | Reference density can bury a newcomer's first useful result | Keep reference links available, give the front page a short product story. |
| [Alacritty](https://alacritty.org/) | A product screenshot and direct platform installers | Clarity is useful, but screenshot-plus-download alone misses Decline's unusual capabilities | Keep install requirements explicit, demonstrate the differences before the reference links. |
| [kitty](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/) | Extensive technical feature and protocol documentation | A feature catalogue asks readers to assemble the reason to switch | Explain a few real tasks in depth, put exhaustive documentation elsewhere. |

A [Hacker News discussion of Warp](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47939992) includes a visitor saying the website did not explain why to choose it, and others describing the product as overwhelming. A [discussion of Ghostty](https://www.reddit.com/r/NixOS/comments/1ho57k2/does_anyone_actually_care_about_ghostty/) questions what “fast” and “feature rich” change in practice. These are qualitative visitor reports, not a representative survey or performance evidence.

The specific failure modes to audit: category adjectives instead of measured speed and footprint; cherry-picked or unlabeled benchmark dates; a screenshot too small to read; a fake shell session that demonstrates nothing; UI chrome that sells a fictional app; platform requirements buried at installation; and “AI” copy that conceals whether the terminal hosts agents or provides an assistant. Avoid reproducing any neighbour's visual identity. Use Decline's existing Slope artwork and dated evidence.

Browser text extraction supports the content observations above; it is not evidence for precise colours, typography or motion on the live sites. No competitor benchmark from web search is used in Decline's copy.
