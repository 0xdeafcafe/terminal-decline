# Round two review protocol

Before editing a concept, read its DESIGN.md, the round brief and the whole
site/ANTI-SLOP.md. Inspect the immutable before PNGs and index.html provided
by the parent, then fix the concept in its own directory. Do not change
shared files or another concept. Do not invent claims or terminal output.

Write SLOP-REVIEW.md with your model and original author's model, all 100
checklist statements verbatim, before and after pass/fail, and concrete
evidence for each verdict. Grouped scores without the item text are not
an audit. Count documented exceptions explicitly: native brand type and
palette, terminal chrome, 13px terminal text, the visible copy command and
any intentionally decorative low-contrast evidence. C100 is deferred for
a concept, not a pass. Don't interpret a checklist score as visual merit.

Before and after captures live in TMPDIR, not Git. Parent captures centrally
at 1440x900 and in a 390px iframe, light/dark, plus tall views. Cite the exact
paths. Review the after images before finalising; request a fresh capture
if a visual fix changes the file. Do not claim to have viewed a screenshot
that does not exist or was captured from an earlier revision.

The actual acceptance test is Alex's feedback: does this have a specific,
recognisable design idea while saying what the product does? A harmless,
carefully documented but bland page has failed this round. Record that
judgement honestly in a short separate paragraph, with its weakest point.
